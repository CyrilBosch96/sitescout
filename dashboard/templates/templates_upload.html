Upload a Template

Upload your Paper AI export directly — the bundler format is unbundled automatically, and any <script> tags are stripped, same as the email reply pipeline. Mark real data spots with a data-slot attribute (e.g. data-slot="business_name") wherever you want live lead data to appear — the system injects its own render script to fill those in, you never write or upload any JS. A separate CSS file is optional. Available fields: business_name, city, phone (use data-slot-href="phone_tel" on a link for a tap-to-call href), hero_line, hours (use data-slot-list="hours" on the container), review_count, review_rating. One confirmation step below before it's buildable.

Missing data: some leads (e.g. from a source other than Google Places) won't have hours, reviews, or a hero line. Those show honest placeholder copy instead of being hidden or faked — never a specific invented fact (no fake hours, no fake star rating). A missing phone hides the call-to-action entirely rather than showing a dead or wrong number. Business name and city are never faked.
